## Software and static distribution

`pnpm licenses:generate` collects full LICENSE/COPYING/NOTICE texts from the installed
production dependency graph, including Nuxt's build tooling. It writes a package
inventory and deduplicated license texts to `public/licenses/`, which are included
in every static build. This deliberately includes more than the browser bundle.
Package names, versions and upstream links are retained; local filesystem paths are not.

When a package omits its license text from npm, the reviewed text is stored in
`licenses/upstream/`, with its source recorded in `sources.json`. For the explicitly
listed packages that declare MIT/ISC only in metadata, the supplement identifies
the declared author and standard SPDX terms without inventing an upstream copyright
statement. The generator fails
for missing texts. Recheck these supplements when upgrading a dependency; a package's
license metadata alone is not a substitute for its copyright and license notices.
Platform-specific build dependencies can differ between macOS and Linux.

## OCR models and language data

The original, unmodified PP-OCRv6 small ONNX archives are distributed in
`public/models/`. Their upstream download URLs, SHA-256 checksums and Apache-2.0
license are recorded in [public/models/README.md](public/models/README.md).

Tesseract's English and Polish trained data are fetched at runtime from
`https://tessdata.projectnaptha.com/4.0.0/`. Upstream:
https://github.com/tesseract-ocr/tessdata/tree/4.0.0 (Apache-2.0).
The Tesseract worker and core are fetched from version-pinned jsDelivr URLs.
These remote resources are not included as binaries in this repository.

## Google Fonts, metadata and generated resources

The catalog is derived from `google-font-metadata@6.0.8` (MIT). Its actual package
copyright and license text are included in the generated license bundle.
The glyph indices and coverage maps are generated measurements of Google Fonts;
they are not redistributed TTF/WOFF2 font programs. Example images are rasterized
text generated by this project's benchmark script.

The app fetches font files directly from `fonts.gstatic.com`, including its interface
fonts DM Sans and Space Grotesk. Font files keep their individual licenses; the app's
MIT license does not replace them. The corresponding family directories and license
files are available at https://github.com/google/fonts. If font binaries are later
mirrored or packaged with the app, include the license and copyright notices for
every mirrored family as part of that distribution.

Google Fonts and the names of the third-party projects identify their respective
upstream projects. This application is an independent project.
